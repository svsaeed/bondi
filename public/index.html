BONDI Rules

Direction: all turns proceed anticlockwise.

Lead: the A♠ holder starts Game 1 but may lead any card. The previous game's BONDI starts the next game.

Follow suit: if you have the led suit, you must play it.

Bondi: the first off-suit card stops the trick immediately. The highest card of the led suit picks up the entire Bustle.

Hingaifi: if everyone follows suit, the Bustle is discarded and the highest led-suit card leads next.

Whole hand: only between tricks, if an adjacent active player has 1–5 cards, the immediately next active player anticlockwise from them may take their whole hand. The player giving the cards finishes immediately.

Match: finish order scores N−1 down to 0. Ten games are played. Equal final totals are tied.
